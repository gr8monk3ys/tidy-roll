## Description
TidyRoll helps you quickly review your photo library with a simple swipe flow:

- Swipe right to Keep
- Swipe left to Delete (staged until you confirm)
- Bookmark photos you are unsure about and review later

Modes:
- On This Day: revisit memories and build a streak
- Recents: start from your newest photos
- Random: shuffle a chunk of recents
- Month-by-month: clean up chronologically with saved progress
- Albums: choose whether swiping left means Delete from library or Remove from album

Privacy-first:
- Photos stay on your device
- No accounts
- No tracking
