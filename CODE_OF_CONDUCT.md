# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and focus on facts.
- Give feedback that is actionable and specific.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Doxxing or sharing private information
- Disruptive or abusive communication

## Reporting

Report conduct issues by opening an issue at
[github.com/gr8monk3ys/tidy-roll/issues](https://github.com/gr8monk3ys/tidy-roll/issues),
or — if the report concerns something private — through the maintainer contact
channels in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit content and may restrict participation
for behavior that violates this code.
