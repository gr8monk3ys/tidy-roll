## Why this exists

The GPL forbids imposing further restrictions on the people you convey the
software to (GPLv3 §10). App store operators — Apple in particular — impose
their own terms on end users, such as device limits, redistribution bans, and
DRM. That is the long-standing tension that saw GPL apps removed from the
App Store.

The copyright holder is not bound by terms they impose on their own licensees,
so a project whose copyright is held by one person can publish to the App Store
without difficulty. That protection disappears the moment outside contributors
hold copyright in part of the code, because each of them would then need to
grant the same permission.

This file grants that permission up front, permanently, for everyone — so the
project can accept contributions **and** ship to app stores.
